# 图标提案 v3：空间切片

新方向：深灰底座、白色球面切片、橙色内侧。两片错开的形体形成 S 的轮廓，呼应 Splat 与空间重建。

母版：[`metorigin-splat-icon-v3.png`](metorigin-splat-icon-v3.png)。使用内置 `image_gen.imagegen` 生成，透明背景；尺寸与 alpha 检查见 [`metadata`](metorigin-splat-icon-v3.metadata.json)。

## 最终提示词

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Design ONE completely new macOS app icon for MetOrigin Splat, a 3D Gaussian Splatting reconstruction tool. The prior bulky shiny orange M-shaped icon was rejected. Start over with an elegant, extremely simple, graphic identity. This is a new design, not an edit or a minor variation of the previous M.

Art direction: a crisp ivory-white spatial symbol on a nearly flat deep charcoal rounded-square tile. The central symbol is a compact tilted orb made from TWO offset, flattened rounded lobes that interlock to suggest a strong abstract S (for Splat). A single broad sweeping diagonal negative-space cut separates them, like a precise cross-section through a sphere. The outer contour stays clean and bold, the center has generous black negative space, with ONE restrained coral-orange inner facet where the two surfaces meet. The orange is an inset surface, not a separate dot. It should communicate a reconstructed volume / Gaussian cross-section, not a database stack, camera lens, infinity symbol, atom or literal cube. No M shape, no detached dot.

Make this feel like a carefully drawn designer's icon: restrained two-and-a-half-dimensional cut-paper / matte sculpted geometry, just enough directional shadow to reveal the spatial cut, almost no gloss, no chrome, no puffy plastic, no inflated ribbons, no droplets, no photographic texture, no grain, no dramatic lighting or glow. The two large light-colored shapes and one small orange inset must be readable at 32px. Clean, calm, confident and slightly unusual. Use clear edges and a precise, economical silhouette; no decorative technical lines or particles.

Composition: single centered finished icon only. Straight-on square canvas, charcoal squircle tile occupying about 84% of the canvas with equal transparent margins, central white symbol about 62% of the tile width. Subtle rim only, flat charcoal face, no heavy bevel or giant outer shadow. True transparent alpha outside the rounded-square tile and its corners; no drawn checkerboard, no external solid background. No labels, no words, no letters rendered as text, no watermark, no grid of options, no presentation mockup. Produce one polished standalone icon asset.
```
